# Horizon Selection Framework

Use this file to score candidates across the three holding horizons.

## Short Term

Target window:

- next trading day to roughly 5 trading days

Look for:

- clear catalyst within 1-5 sessions
- clean tradability at regular open or pullback trigger
- strong recent relative strength and volume confirmation
- nearby invalidation level

Avoid:

- halted or clearly untradable names
- illiquid names where exact entry is not believable
- stale-data narratives

## Medium Term

Target window:

- roughly 2 to 12 weeks

Look for:

- earnings momentum or estimate revision path
- supportive sector trend
- 20-60 session base, breakout, or relative strength
- catalysts expected in the next quarter

Avoid:

- one-day rumor spikes without follow-through
- weak balance-sheet names without catalyst path
- insufficient 20-60 session structure

## Long Term

Target window:

- roughly 6 to 24 months

Look for:

- durable business quality
- sound balance sheet and cash generation
- reasonable valuation range
- thesis resilient to normal volatility

Avoid:

- crowded speculation without business support
- structurally weak businesses with no durable edge
- leverage stress without clear repair path

## Evidence Threshold

Each recommended symbol should include:

- one price/history reason
- one catalyst reason
- one disclosure/fundamental reason

If a candidate misses one of these, lower confidence or remove it.
